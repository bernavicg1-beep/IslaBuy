Account Deletion — IslaBuy: Camiguin Edition

To request deletion of your IslaBuy account and associated data, please follow these steps:

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the IslaBuy app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Account Deletion.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account, listings, messages, and associated media will be deleted. Some data may be retained for legal or security purposes for up to 30 days.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include the email or phone number associated with your account.

Data that will be deleted

  • User profile and authentication data
  • All product listings and associated Cloudinary media
  • Messages and conversations
  • Store information
  • Notification history

Contact

For questions, contact YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE.
